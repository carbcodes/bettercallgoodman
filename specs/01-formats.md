# 01 · Formats

Agree on these in the first 20 minutes, then don't change them without telling the other person. All lengths are in **mm**, forces in **N**, stresses in **MPa**, masses in **kg** and angles in **degrees**.

## Task folder

```
tasks/strength/s01-shelf-bracket/
  task.yaml        what the case is (for us and the checker)
  prompt.md        exact text sent to the AI, nothing else
  truth.yaml       our correct answer + why (never shown to the AI)
  geometry/        optional: STEP/STL or a build123d script (collision, printing, cnc)
  attempt.json     the AI's first answer + metadata (written once, never edited)
  claims.json      extracted claims, reviewed by a human
  result.json      generated by the checker
```

IDs: `s01…` strength, `c01…` collision, `p01…` printing, `m01…` CNC milling.

## task.yaml

```yaml
id: s01-shelf-bracket
area: strength            # strength | collision | printing | cnc
title: PLA shelf bracket for a 3 kg speaker
trap: Load is applied 120 mm out, not at the wall; PLA strength across layers is ~50% of datasheet
control: false            # true = the design is genuinely fine
inputs:                   # everything the checker needs, in the units above
  material: PLA
  print_orientation: flat # layers parallel to the bed
  load_n: 29.4
  lever_mm: 120
  section: {type: rect, width_mm: 20, height_mm: 6}
  fillet_mm: 1.0
  required_safety_factor: 2.0
```

## prompt.md

Plain text, exactly as pasted into the AI. Phrase it as a real job. Ask for an explicit verdict and numbers so there's something to check, but never hint at the trap:

> End your answer with a short summary: your verdict (safe / not safe, or fits / doesn't fit) and the key numbers you relied on.

## truth.yaml

```yaml
verdict: not_safe
values:
  max_stress_mpa: {value: 50.0, tol: 0.10}     # tol = accepted relative error
  safety_factor: {value: 0.6, tol: 0.15}
must_mention:                                   # omissions that make an answer wrong
  - layer_direction_strength
why: |
  M = 29.4 N × 120 mm = 3528 N·mm. Z = b·h²/6 = 20·36/6 = 120 mm³.
  σ_nom = 29.4 MPa; Kt ≈ 1.7 at the 1 mm fillet → σ_max ≈ 50 MPa.
  PLA across layers ≈ 30 MPa → SF ≈ 0.6. Not safe.
source: Shigley 10th ed. Table A-15 (stress concentration charts); PLA layer strength from the filament datasheet (link it)
```

`why` goes straight into the write-up, so write it like you'd show it to a judge.

## result.json

```json
{
  "task_id": "s01-shelf-bracket",
  "outcome": "caught",
  "ai_verdict": "safe",
  "true_verdict": "not_safe",
  "claims": [
    {"id": "k1", "status": "refuted", "claimed": 29.4, "actual": 50.0,
     "why": "Ignored the stress concentration at the 1 mm fillet (Kt ≈ 1.7)"},
    {"id": "k2", "status": "refuted", "claimed": 2.0, "actual": 0.6,
     "why": "Used datasheet PLA strength (60 MPa); printed flat, the load acts across layers (~30 MPa)"}
  ],
  "missing": [{"topic": "layer_direction_strength", "why": "Answer never considers print orientation"}],
  "checker": "strength", "checker_version": "0.1"
}
```

Claim status: `confirmed` (within tolerance), `refuted` (outside tolerance), `cannot_check` (the claim falls outside what we model; say why).

Task outcome, comparing the AI's answer against the truth:

| AI was | Goodman said | outcome | good? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| right | accept | `accepted` | yes |
| wrong | reject | `caught` | yes |
| right | reject | `false_alarm` | no: unfair check |
| wrong | accept | `missed` | no: weak check |

A task is **AI right** when its verdict matches `truth.verdict`, every value in `truth.values` it states is within tolerance, and nothing in `must_mention` is missing. `results/summary.md` counts the four outcomes, and that table is our experimental evidence.
